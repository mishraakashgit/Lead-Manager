# Outreach and prototype demo script

## 1. First DM

Personalise the product. Don't mention software or ask them to try anything.

> Hi! Love your [specific product] 😊 Quick question: when people DM asking about price and then go quiet, do you follow up with them, or just let it go? Trying to understand how small sellers handle this.

Where to send it, best first:
1. Sellers you've bought from (they already know you)
2. Seller WhatsApp and Facebook groups (ask one question in the group, then DM people who answer)
3. Local craft fairs and exhibitions (talk in person, take the Instagram handle)
4. Cold Instagram DMs

## 2. If they reply

Ask one at a time. Ask about the past, not the future.

- How often do people ask and then go quiet without buying? Last week, roughly how many?
- Do you follow up? How do you remember who to follow up with?
- During busy times like Diwali, do you ever miss replying to DMs?
- What do you use now to keep track of orders? (notebook, notes app, Excel, WhatsApp labels, nothing)

Log the answers in `research-log.md` the same day.

## 3. Showing the prototype

Only show it when a seller has confirmed the pain. Send the link with:

> I made a rough screen to see if it would help with this. It has sample customers, nothing real. Could you open it and tell me what's confusing? Takes 2 minutes.

What to ask them to do:
1. Look at the first screen. Ask: "What do you think this screen is telling you?"
2. Tap **+** and add one real enquiry from their DMs today. Note how many seconds it shows. The target is about 10.
3. Tap **Follow up** on Riya and look at the message. Ask: "Would you send this? What would you change?"
4. Open **Pipeline**. Ask: "Are these the steps your orders go through? What's missing?"

Then ask:
- "If this was ready tomorrow, would you use it this week? For which customers?"
- "What do you do today instead?"

Write down their exact words. A seller who asks "when can I use it?" or "can I add my own customers?" without prompting counts as pull. "Looks nice" doesn't.

Don't discuss price until several sellers confirm the pain. When you do, ask what they pay for today (Canva, Shiprocket, Instagram ads) before naming ₹299–999.

## 4. Optional value-first offer

> I put together a free Google Sheet for tracking enquiries and orders. Want me to send it?

Sellers who use the sheet are the easiest people to show the prototype to later. Their columns tell you which fields matter.
