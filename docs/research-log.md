# Seller research log

Log every conversation, including no-replies. The question we're answering:

> Do small Instagram sellers lose sales because customers who asked about price or availability go quiet and nobody follows up?

## Decision rule (set this before the answers come in)

Review after 2–3 weeks or 25 DMs sent, whichever comes first.

| Signal | Counts as | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Seller says people often ask and then go quiet | Pain exists | ≥ 5 sellers |
| Seller admits they don't follow up, or forget who to follow up with | Pain is unsolved | ≥ 4 of those 5 |
| Seller tracks enquiries somewhere today (notebook, notes app, Excel, saved chats) | They already spend effort | ≥ 2 |
| Seller asks "can I use it?" after seeing the prototype | Pull, not politeness | ≥ 3 |

- All four met: build the MVP.
- Pain exists but nobody wants to try the prototype: keep talking, change the pitch or the segment (for example, only gifting sellers before Diwali and weddings).
- Fewer than 5 confirm the pain: revisit Idea 1 (inventory and orders), using the Sunmeek Seeds notes below.

Compliments don't count ("nice idea", "looks good"). Only count what sellers say about what they actually did last week.
